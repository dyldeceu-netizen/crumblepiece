Privacy

Short version: no cookies, no tracking, no ads. Just cats.

What this site stores

When you pet Ziggy, the number of pets and whether sound is on are saved in your own browser (localStorage). They never leave your device. Clear your browser data and Ziggy forgets, too.

Analytics

None. I don't use analytics, trackers or third-party scripts. The fonts are hosted on this site.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to keep the service running and secure. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Links to other platforms

The buttons take you to YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. Once you're there, their own privacy policies apply.
